    Privacy

    🔒 Your notes are saved only on this device (in the browser’s storage). Nothing is uploaded.
    The only exception: if you turn on cloud AI, each new or edited entry (its text, drawing or image) and your searches are sent to Anthropic to be tagged. Turn it off and nothing leaves the device.
    The text reader downloads once from the internet; your notes are never sent with it.
